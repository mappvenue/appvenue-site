Heartprint privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · Applies to the Heartprint Android app (package io.appvenue.chatwrapped).

Who we are

Heartprint is published by Appvenue Teknoloji Ltd. Şti. ("we"). Address: Fenerbahçe Mah. İğrip Sk. No: 13/1, Kadıköy / İstanbul, Türkiye. Contact: hi@appvenue.io.

Your chats stay on your phone

You choose a chat file (an export from WhatsApp or a similar app) with your phone's file picker. Heartprint reads it on your phone, works out the results (counts, times, words, emojis) on your phone, and keeps the results in the app's private storage on your phone. The text of your chats, the names and the phone numbers in them are never sent to us or to anyone else. Our servers never receive them, and there is no account to sign in to.

Chats contain other people's messages too. Please only import chats you are part of. To delete everything the app stored, open Settings and choose "Erase Your Data", or uninstall the app.

What leaves your phone

Only technical and advertising data that our service providers' tools collect. None of it contains your chat content.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos or chat content. We do not sell personal data. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card details.

Your choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland: a consent message appears when you first open the app. Ads, ad measurement by Meta and TikTok, and ad-related analytics start only after you agree. You can change your choice later in Settings, "Privacy choices".
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Subscriptions are managed or cancelled in Google Play at any time.

Messages from us

If you allow notifications, Heartprint can show you messages from us, for example about new features, tips for your chats, or an offer for Premium. We choose who gets a message from the data in the table above (for example, people on the Free plan who have imported a chat). We never send messages between 22:00 and 09:00 on your phone, and at most one a day. A message that could not be sent in time is dropped after two days.

You can stop them at any time: turn off notifications for Heartprint in your phone's settings. They stop showing right away, and at your next app open our record is updated so we stop sending. You can also use Settings > Erase Your Data in the app, which deletes our record of your phone.

Legal bases (EEA, UK)

Consent for advertising, ad measurement and the related analytics. Contract for the subscription you buy. Our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable for crash reports. Your consent for messages from us, which you give by allowing notifications and withdraw by turning them off.

Transfers outside your country

Our providers (Google, Meta, TikTok, RevenueCat, Supabase) may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under their own safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you erase it or uninstall.
  • Firebase Analytics: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: about 90 days at Google.
  • Notification records (token, language, plan, imported-chat count, version, time zone, last app open): until you have not opened the app for 180 days, or until you delete them with Settings > Erase Your Data or ask us to. Records of which message was sent to which phone: 12 months.
  • Purchase records (Google Play, RevenueCat): as long as needed to run your subscription and meet legal duties.
  • Ads and ad measurement (Google, Meta, TikTok): under each company's own policy.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or object to its use. Because we have no accounts, the data we hold is linked to your phone only. Open Settings and choose "Erase Your Data" to delete it from the app and from our server, or write to hi@appvenue.io. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Heartprint is not designed for children under 18.

Independent app

Heartprint is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to WhatsApp or Meta Platforms. The recap results are made for fun and are not advice about any relationship.
